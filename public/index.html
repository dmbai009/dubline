Video sharing between participants (P2P)

DubLine can send parts of videos directly to other participants in your room. This speeds up downloads and reduces the load on the host.

P2P uses your outgoing internet traffic. Participants in a direct connection may learn your IP address.

You can disable P2P in Settings at any time.

P2P remains off because your choice could not be saved. Check browser storage and try again.
